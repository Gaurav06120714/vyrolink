# Snapchat — research report

**Accessed:** 2026-10-15. **Confidence:** Low–medium (secondary sources only; no current official technical documentation found).

| Item | Finding | Label |
|---|---|---|
| Snaps (photo/video) | E2EE since about 2019 | REPORTED (Social Samosa 2019, Comparitech) |
| Text chats / groups | **Not E2EE** as of an April 2026 fact-check (which itself notes it may be outdated) | REPORTED (factually.co) |
| Ephemerality | Default deletion after viewing; screenshot notification | REPORTED |

**Lesson:** ephemerality is **not** security. Screenshot notifications are advisory, and VYRO LINK will not claim screenshot prevention. iOS cannot block screenshots; Android `FLAG_SECURE` is best-effort (see 03).
