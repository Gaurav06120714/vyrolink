# Instagram Direct Messages — research report

**Accessed:** 2026-10-15. **Confidence:** Medium–high (consistent multi-outlet reporting of Meta's Help Center notice).

## 1. Findings

| Item | Finding | Label |
|---|---|---|
| E2EE | Optional E2EE chats existed from 2023, were never on by default, and were limited to some regions. **Meta ended support on 2026-05-08**, citing low use; users were prompted to download affected chats | REPORTED (9to5Google 2026-03-13, AlternativeTo, Gulf News) |
| Features | Replies, reactions, voice notes, vanish mode, unsend | REPORTED (common knowledge, not re-fetched) |

## 2. Lesson

Opt-in E2EE gets little use and can be withdrawn. VYRO LINK makes encryption **unconditional**: there is no unencrypted mode to fall back to.
