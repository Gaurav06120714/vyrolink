# FireChat (historical, discontinued) — research report

**Accessed:** 2026-10-15. **Confidence:** Medium for history; the shutdown reason is UNKNOWN.

| Item | Finding | Label |
|---|---|---|
| Use | Off-grid mesh chat, heavily used in the 2014 Hong Kong protests (100k+ sign-ups reported); later used in Iraq, Ecuador, Catalonia and India | REPORTED (CNN Money 2014, City A.M., VentureBeat) |
| Range | Roughly 40–70 m per hop (reports vary) | REPORTED |
| Security | Initially unencrypted public chat; E2EE added in about 2015 | REPORTED |
| Status | Discontinued; no app updates since about 2018 | REPORTED (Wikipedia mirror; 2024 essay) |

**Lessons:**
- Mesh usefulness depends on **density**. That is why VYRO LINK's value comes from *store-and-forward over time*, not instant multi-hop reach.
- Shipping public unencrypted chat first created lasting risk. VYRO LINK has no unencrypted user messages at all.
