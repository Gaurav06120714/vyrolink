How it works

A message moves each time two phones meet.

There is no tower and no server in the middle. Each phone holds sealed messages and hands copies to the next phone it meets, until one reaches the person it is for.

  1. Pair in person

    You and your friend scan each other's QR code once. That is how your phones learn each other's keys. No number or account is involved.

  2. Write with no signal

    The message is encrypted on your phone the moment you send it, and waits there until another phone comes near.

  3. Phones carry it

    Nearby phones running VYRO LINK swap sealed copies over Bluetooth. They can't read them or see who they're from, and they delete them when they expire.

  4. Delivered means delivered

    When the message reaches your friend's phone, it sends back a signed receipt the same way. Only then does your phone say Delivered.

Honest delivery

It tells you what it knows, and nothing more.

Offline delivery can take minutes, hours, or not happen at all. Instead of a tick that guesses, every message shows one of five states that it can actually prove.

  • Waiting for nearby devicesEncrypted and saved on your phone.
  • Accepted by a relayAnother phone took a sealed copy to pass on.
  • DeliveredYour friend's phone confirmed it with a signed receipt.
  • Delivery unknownCopies were handed on, but no receipt came back in time.
  • ExpiredNobody carried it before it ran out of time. You can send it again.

Your ID

An ID nobody handed out, and nobody can copy.

Your phone creates a secret key the first time you open VYRO LINK. Your 32-character ID is made from that key, so no two people share one and nobody can fake yours. Your friends see the same ID on your contact card, so you can check you paired with the right person.

Limits

What VYRO LINK doesn't do, on purpose or by physics.

  • It isn't instant

    A message moves only when phones meet. Across a busy campus that can be minutes. Across a quiet town it can be hours, or never.

  • No calls or video

    Bluetooth between passing phones can't carry live audio. Attachments are small for the same reason.

  • No cloud backup

    Messages live only on your phone. If you lose it or wipe it, they are gone. That is the trade for nobody else holding them.

  • No read receipts or “last seen”

    They would leak when you are around. You only learn that a message arrived.

  • Phones limit background Bluetooth

    iPhone and Android decide how much an app may run in the background. If you force-quit the app, it stops carrying messages.

  • Not yet audited

    It uses well-known, published encryption, but no independent security review has been done yet. We'll say so until one has.

Questions

Frequently asked

Does it really work with no internet at all?

Yes. No SIM, no Wi-Fi and no mobile data. Phones talk to each other directly over Bluetooth. Pairing happens face to face with a QR code, so even adding a contact needs no connection.

Can the phones that carry my message read it?

No. A message is encrypted for your friend's phone before it leaves yours. Carriers hold a sealed copy that doesn't show who sent it or who it is for, and they delete it when it expires.

How far does it reach?

Bluetooth usually reaches a few rooms or across a street, depending on the phones and what's in between. Distance is covered by people moving, not by range: every phone you pass can carry a message a step further.

Why not just use a normal messenger?

You should, when you have signal. VYRO LINK is for when you don't: festivals, hikes, basements, campus dead zones, outages, or anywhere the network is down or not trusted.

When can I download it?

Not yet. The app runs and passes its tests, but it still needs testing between real phones before it goes on the App Store and Google Play.

Privacy policy

We don't collect your data, because we never receive it.

Effective 16 October 2026 · Applies to the VYRO LINK apps for iPhone and Android and to this website.

In short: VYRO LINK has no accounts and no servers. Your messages, contacts and keys stay on your phone, encrypted. We, the developers, cannot see any of it.

What we collect

Nothing. The app does not send your messages, contacts, ID, location, device identifiers or usage data to us or to anyone else. It contains no analytics, advertising or tracking code.

What stays on your phone

  • Messages, contacts and settings are kept in an encrypted database. Its key is protected by your phone's secure storage (Keychain on iPhone, Keystore on Android).
  • Your identity key is created on your phone and never leaves it. App data is excluded from device and cloud backups.
  • Photos and files you send are re-encoded or read only when you pick them. Photo location data is removed before sending.

What other phones see

  • Phones that carry your messages hold sealed copies they cannot read. A copy does not show your name, your ID or who it is for, and it is deleted when it expires.
  • Nearby phones see only a fixed VYRO LINK Bluetooth signal, with no name or ID in it.
  • Your contacts see the name and ID you chose to share when you paired with them.

Permissions

  • Bluetooth: to exchange messages with nearby phones.
  • Camera: only to scan a pairing QR code. Camera images are not saved.
  • Notifications: to say “You have a new message”, with no sender or text.
  • Face ID, Touch ID or fingerprint: only if you turn on app lock. Your phone checks it; the app never sees your biometrics.

VYRO LINK does not ask for your location.

Deleting your data

Because everything is stored only on your phone, you are in control of it. Delete a message or a contact in the app, use Panic wipe in Settings to erase everything, or uninstall the app. There is nothing for us to delete, because we never had it.

This website

This page sets no cookies and loads nothing from other companies: no fonts, scripts or analytics. Our hosting provider may keep standard server logs, such as IP addresses, for security.

Changes
